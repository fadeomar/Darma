# Darma Community Conduct

Darma welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and working styles.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and changes rather than people. Do not use harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption. Do not publish private information or security details without permission.

When reviewing beginner contributions, explain the reason for requested changes and provide a practical next step. When disagreeing about technical content, separate verifiable evidence from preference and company-specific practice.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict repeated harmful participation. Serious or sensitive concerns should be reported privately through the repository owner's available contact or security channel.
