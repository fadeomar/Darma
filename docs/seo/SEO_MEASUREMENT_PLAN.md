# Darma SEO Measurement Plan

Ranking first is not a reliable delivery metric because rankings differ by query, location, device, competition, and time. Darma will measure whether useful pages are discovered, understood, selected, and used.

## Primary outcomes

1. **Qualified organic discovery** — impressions and clicks for topics Darma genuinely covers.
2. **Editorial usefulness** — readers continue to a learning path, resource, career, workflow, or contribution action.
3. **Index quality** — canonical editorial pages are indexed while internal search and faceted duplicates are excluded.
4. **Authority growth** — relevant sites and communities reference Darma naturally.
5. **Content freshness** — important pages are reviewed on schedule and broken references are handled safely.

## Baseline dashboard

Track monthly by page type:

- Indexed pages / submitted canonical pages
- Organic impressions and clicks
- Click-through rate
- Median query position, interpreted with caution
- Non-branded versus branded queries
- Guide-to-path and comparison-to-resource transitions
- Returning visitors to Atlas pages
- External referring domains to editorial pages
- Pages with no impressions after an appropriate discovery period
- Core Web Vitals pass rate

## Page-level review rules

Review a page when:

- It receives impressions but unusually few clicks: test title, description, and intent alignment.
- It ranks near the first page: improve completeness, internal links, and original practical value.
- It receives clicks but readers do not continue: improve next actions and page structure.
- It loses visibility after a technical change: inspect canonical, indexability, rendering, sitemap, and internal links first.
- It becomes outdated: perform a real editorial review and update the review date.

## Reporting cadence

- **Weekly during the first eight weeks:** indexing, errors, newly discovered queries, and major movement.
- **Monthly:** page-type performance, content opportunities, internal navigation, links, and review backlog.
- **Quarterly:** topic coverage, audience needs, multilingual readiness, and whether low-value pages should be consolidated.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish pages only to increase indexed URL count.
- Do not purchase links or automate unsolicited link placement.
- Do not change dates without meaningful review.
- Do not use FAQ or other structured data when the visible page does not contain the same content.
- Do not treat one tool's SEO score as the business outcome.
